UC & Ayurveda Guide

Managing UC Through Diet

  • Eat smaller, frequent meals - Easier on digestion
  • Stay hydrated - Aim for 8+ glasses of water daily
  • Cook vegetables well - Raw can irritate during flares
  • Keep a food diary - Track triggers specific to you
  • Low-residue during flares - Reduce fiber temporarily
  • Omega-3 fatty acids - Anti-inflammatory benefits

Ayurveda & Ulcerative Colitis

In Ayurveda, UC is often associated with aggravated Pitta dosha (fire element). The goal is to pacify Pitta through cooling, soothing foods.

Pitta-Pacifying Principles:

  • Favor cooling foods - Cucumber, coconut, mint, coriander
  • Sweet, bitter, astringent tastes - These calm Pitta
  • Avoid spicy, sour, salty - These aggravate Pitta
  • Ghee (clarified butter) - Healing for gut lining
  • Aloe vera juice - Cooling and healing
  • Turmeric with ghee - Anti-inflammatory
  • Eat at regular times - Pitta thrives on routine

Foods to Limit or Avoid

🚫 High Risk During Flares

  • Raw vegetables & salads
  • Nuts and seeds (whole)
  • Spicy foods (chili, hot sauce)
  • Dairy (except ghee & yogurt for some)
  • Caffeine & alcohol
  • Fried & fatty foods
  • High-fiber grains

⚠️ Common Triggers

  • Gluten (for some)
  • Corn & popcorn
  • Beans & legumes
  • Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cabbage)
  • Citrus fruits
  • Artificial sweeteners

UC-Friendly Foods

✅ Generally Safe

  • White rice & rice porridge
  • Cooked carrots, squash, zucchini
  • Bananas (ripe)
  • Lean proteins (chicken, fish, eggs)
  • Bone broth
  • Ghee
  • Oatmeal (well-cooked)

🌿 Anti-Inflammatory Stars

  • Salmon & fatty fish (omega-3)
  • Turmeric (with black pepper)
  • Ginger (cooked)
  • Olive oil
  • Avocado (ripe)
  • Blueberries
